# Code of Conduct

We want a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone who takes part in this
project, whatever their background or experience.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, give and accept
feedback kindly, keep discussions on topic, credit other people's work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may
temporarily or permanently ban people who do. Report problems privately to the
maintainers through the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
